# Favorability model

Favorability is a separate opinion measure for every active presidential candidate. Each county has favorable, unfavorable, and no opinion shares summing to 100. The top bar displays the player's voter-weighted national net (favorable minus unfavorable). The U.S. favorability map colors states by net; the unfavorability map colors states by unfavorable share. Opening a state shows its county values. State and national summaries weight counties by registered voters when available, so a large rural county on the map can contribute fewer people than a small urban county.

The regular margin map and county breakdown use the current weekly snapshot. Its statewide color follows the published state polling projection, while county colors follow modeled local estimates from that same week. Sampling variation can make the state poll differ from a direct aggregate of county estimates.

The 2028 starting numbers in `js/favorability.js` are **scenario estimates**, not survey readings or election forecasts. Public national polling informed their relative order and familiarity. [YouGov's second-quarter 2026 ratings](https://yougov.com/en-us/ratings/Democrats) report positive-opinion and fame measures for Harris, Ocasio-Cortez, Kelly, Walz, Booker, Buttigieg, Newsom, and Whitmer. The [December 2025 Economist/YouGov poll](https://yougov.com/en-us/articles/53799-negativity-toward-political-parties-politicians-pervasive-especially-sharp-among-democrats-december-26-29-2025-economist-yougov-poll) measured negative net ratings for Newsom, Ocasio-Cortez, Vance, Rubio, and Trump. [Gallup's 2024 comparison](https://news.gallup.com/poll/548138/american-presidential-candidates-2024-election-favorable-ratings.aspx) shows how lower awareness affects no-opinion responses. Individual entries without comparable national measurements remain design assumptions. Beshear gets a distinctive Kentucky home-state rating and rural coalition effect, reflecting his [official 2023 reelection](https://elect.ky.gov/results/2020-2029/Documents/Certification%20of%20Election%20Results%20for%202023%20General%20Election%20Final.pdf).

County opinion combines the candidate baseline with the starting partisan climate, the candidate's existing group effects counted once, modeled issue stances, home-state recognition, and a bounded vice presidential effect. Actions add persistent national, state, or county changes by candidate. Ads have the largest routine opinion effects: positive and issue ads improve the sponsor, while attacks reduce the target's favorability. Exposure can turn no opinion into a decided opinion. Issue position changes and scandals also move opinion. Opinion changes do not directly change vote choice; vote movement comes through the campaign action's persuasion and turnout effects. This avoids a feedback loop between vote margins and favorability.

Endorsements begin unearned. An individual endorser grants no surrogate rallies when endorsing; a later meeting with that supporter grants one or two. The AI has its own endorser relationships and rally credits and spends from its own budget and weekly energy allowance.

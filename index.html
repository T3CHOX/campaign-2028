ABOUT THE PROJECT

What is Decision 2028?

Decision 2028 is a county-level presidential campaign simulator built from the ground up with real demographic, voter registration, and electoral data. It is not a prediction tool — it is a strategic sandbox that lets you experience the mechanics of a modern American presidential race at a granularity that no other browser-based simulator offers.

Every county in the United States has its own demographic composition, partisan lean, and media market assignment. Your campaign decisions — from rally locations to TV ad buys to endorsement meetings — ripple through the electorate in ways that mirror real campaign dynamics. Turnout models are population-weighted. Margin shifts follow demographic logic. Third-party spoiler effects are modeled. Maine and Nebraska split their electoral votes by congressional district.

This is an ongoing, open-source project. The simulation engine is under active development, with new mechanics, deeper AI opponent logic, and expanded scenarios being added on a regular basis. If you're interested in the intersection of political science, data visualization, and game design, you're in the right place.

CORE MECHANICS

How the Simulation Works

Rally System

Hold rallies in individual counties. Turnout boosts spill into neighboring counties with distance-based decay. Consecutive rallies in the same location face diminishing returns.

TV Advertising

Purchase biographical, attack, or issue ads that broadcast across entire media markets. Each ad type produces distinct strategic effects on margin and turnout.

Endorsements & Surrogates

Court national and state-level endorsers. Endorsed surrogates can be deployed on the trail, amplifying your rally impact in the states where they carry influence.

Demographic Coalitions

Every county has a unique demographic fingerprint. Your candidate's appeal to specific groups — white working class, Black voters, suburban women, evangelicals — shapes the race at the county level.

Interest Groups & PACs

Navigate relationships with organized labor, the NRA, environmental groups, and more. Their support — or opposition — shifts the landscape in targeted regions.

Live Election Night

Watch results roll in county by county on election night. The sidebar tracks electoral vote allocations in real time as states are called.
